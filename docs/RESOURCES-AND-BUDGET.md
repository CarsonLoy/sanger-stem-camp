# Resources and Budget

## Important context

Sanger STEM Camp took place inside an established high-school engineering classroom. The team already had access to equipment and supplies that many organizers will not have.

The website must not claim that the camp’s Amazon purchases represent the complete cost of starting from nothing.

Show two budget totals:

1. **What we spent:** the additional purchases made specifically for this camp.
2. **Starting from scratch:** an estimated total for organizers who do not already own the required supplies or equipment.

## Acquisition categories

### Buy for this camp

Products purchased specifically or recommended as exact items.

### Check your inventory

Common supplies a classroom, robotics shop, office, or makerspace may already own.

### Specialized equipment

Expensive or institutional resources. Provide alternatives or activity substitutions rather than simply adding them to the normal shopping cart.

### Source locally or collect

Common materials where an Amazon link is unnecessary:

- cardboard
- straws
- two-liter bottles
- paper
- cups
- recyclable materials

For two-liter bottles, recommend:

- asking students to bring clean bottles
- collecting them for several weeks
- asking staff and families to save them
- purchasing inexpensive soda only when necessary

### Optional upgrade

Items that improve personalization, speed, reliability, or presentation but are not essential.

## Cost presentation rules

For each item display:

- required quantity for 20 students
- package quantity
- purchase price and date captured
- amount consumed
- amount left over
- reusable or consumable
- activity
- acquisition category
- generic specification so the guide survives listing changes

The guide should not require the exact linked brand unless a product characteristic matters.
