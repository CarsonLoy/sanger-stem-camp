# Domain and Branding

## Recommended choice

### Site name

**Sanger STEM Camp Playbook**

### Domain

**SangerSTEMCamp.org**

### Homepage wording

> Sanger STEM Camp Playbook  
> Everything you need to plan, purchase, prepare, and run a hands-on three-day STEM camp.

## Why this works

- Keeps the exact name of the real camp.
- “Playbook” immediately communicates that the site is instructional.
- The domain remains short and memorable.
- The site can document the original Sanger camp while helping people elsewhere reproduce it.

## Avoid

**Sanger STEM** by itself is too broad and may be confused with unrelated uses of “Sanger” and “STEM.”

## Domain note

Confirm availability and final renewal price with a registrar before purchasing. Domain-search results and advertised first-year prices are not authoritative until checkout.
